# Bloodline seals — the ladder, and what OG Jackpot actually is

**Status: LIVE since 2026-09-17.** They were paused 2026-09-12 to 2026-09-17 while the ladder was
rebuilt. Any older note saying the seals are hidden, or telling you not to name a player's tier, is out
of date. The **Breeder's Cup is still OFF** and its scores should not be quoted.

## The one sentence

A seal grades a foal's **TRUE BIRTH internals**, never the numbers printed on its card. 45 is the birth
ceiling. The rare band ("Band 1", ~1 in 32) adds **+5 to all three internals at once** and prints the
card **lower** to hide it. Because it lifts all three together, **the number of 50s a horse has equals
the number of stats its pairing brought to the 45 wall.** That is the whole ladder.

## The ladder, best first

| rung | born with | mark |
|---|---|---|
| **Triple Anomaly** | three 50s | inquiry flag, 3 pennants |
| **Double Anomaly** | two 50s | inquiry flag, 2 pennants |
| **Anomaly** | exactly one 50 | inquiry flag, 1 pennant |
| **Prodigy** | 46–49, no 50 | cyan crown |
| **Trifecta** | 45 / 45 / 45 | green, 3 horseshoes |
| **Elite** | two at 45 | gold, 2 horseshoes |
| **Blue Chip** | one at 45 | blue, 1 horseshoe |
| **The Field** | nothing at 45 | **no seal at all** — correct, not a bug |

Flags count 50s; horseshoes count 45s. More beats fewer.

## Names that are retired — do not use them

- **"Anomaly 150"** and **"Perfect 150"** → both are now **Triple Anomaly**.
- **The gold star** the top rung used to wear is retired. Do not describe a star.
- **"Anomaly" used to mean any horse carrying a 50.** Since 2026-09-17 it means *exactly one*.
  Two is Double Anomaly, three is Triple Anomaly.
- **"Jackpot"** was renamed **Trifecta** on 2026-07-18, to stop it colliding with the arcade crowd's
  own use of "jackpot".

## OG Jackpot is a TAG, not a rung

It is **not a tier and never outranks anything**. It is a small chip reading the letters **OG**, worn
beside a **Triple Anomaly's** seal and nowhere else. Its shade says which shape that horse's CARD
prints:

- **deeper purple** → the card prints **45 / 45 / 33**
- **lighter purple** → the card prints **40 / 40 / 40**

It used to be drawn as purple/indigo discs carrying horseshoes, and used to be treated as its own rung.
All of that is retired: **do not describe indigo, horseshoes, or an "OG tier."**

## The fact the old arcade crowd most wants

**A card printing 45/45/33 or 40/40/40 IS a 50/50/50 horse underneath.** Those two shapes arise one way
only: a 45/45/45 base that Band 1 lifted to 50/50/50 while dipping the print. The arcade community named
these decades ago without knowing why they happened — they had it right.

## Provisional grades

A horse that **has raced**, has **no birth-ledger row**, but whose card shows one of those two prints is
graded **Triple Anomaly, flagged provisional** — read from the card rather than proven from the ledger.
Provisional is a level of proof, not a lower tier.

## What leaves no trace

**A one-fifty or two-fifty horse has NO printed tell.** Nothing on the card gives it away. Those two
rungs are readable only from the birth record, which is exactly why the arcade-era test could never name
them. If a player asks why their card looks ordinary on a Double Anomaly, this is the answer.

## Terminology map for the old crowd

- their **"jackpot" / "bingo"** (the fully maxed horse) → our **Triple Anomaly**
- their **"OG Jackpot"** (the 45/45/33 or 40/40/40 card) → our **OG tag**, worn on a Triple Anomaly
- our **Trifecta** → a 45/45/45 *birth*, which is not what they meant by jackpot

If someone says "jackpot", ask which they mean.
