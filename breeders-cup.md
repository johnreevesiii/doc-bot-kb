# The Breeder's Cup (v2) — how it scores

**Status: LIVE since 2026-09-27** at play.johnreevesiii.com/breeders, open to everyone (no sign-in needed to
read it). It was paused 2026-09-12 to 2026-09-27 and came back rebuilt. Any older note saying the Cup is off,
that it only counts horses with 10+ races, or that it scores birth seals, is out of date.

## The one rule

Every point comes from a race run at a house cabinet by a horse **you bred**, whoever owns it now. Rerolling
in the Breeding Lab can get you a better horse, but only racing earns points.

## Points

| For each horse you bred | Points |
|---|---|
| Win | 3 |
| Place (2nd) | 2 |
| Show (3rd) | 1 |
| G1 title (each title once, the way the card stores it) | 10 |

- **Bloodline (line) credit:** when a foal of a horse you bred earns points, you get a quarter of them
  (rounded down) for each of its parents you bred. The foal's own breeder still gets the full amount.
  Example: your mare's foal earns 40 points; you bred the mare, so you get 10 (20 if you bred both parents).
- **Seal bonus:** paid ONCE, when a committed foal wins its first race. Triple Anomaly 250, Double Anomaly
  180, Anomaly 140, Prodigy 90, Trifecta 50, Elite 30, Blue Chip 10. A 50 that can't be reproduced from the
  parents on record ("unconfirmed") pays half: 125 / 90 / 70.
- **Committed** means decided before the foal existed: a Lab foal NOMINATED before it was rolled, or a foal
  born on a house cabinet and autosaved to the breeder at birth (cabinet foal autosave began 2026-08-25;
  those foals can't be culled or rerolled). Older cabinet births and self-hosted saves could be discarded
  unsaved, so they never pay a seal bonus. Every other foal still scores by racing, just without the bonus.

## Nominations (Lab foals)

- In the **Breeding Lab** (Winner's Circle) or the free **Starter Lab** (Rev C), tick **"Enter this foal in
  the Breeder's Cup"** BEFORE pressing breed. The foal that comes back is the entry, whatever it rolls. A
  nomination can't be moved to a different horse afterwards.
- Allowance per monthly season: 1 free, plus 1 for every 25 cabinet races that stable's saves record that
  month, up to 6. (0-24 races = 1, 25 = 2, 50 = 3, 75 = 4, 100 = 5, 125+ = 6.) The Cup page and the Lab show
  how many are left and how many races until the next one.
- Out of nominations = the roll is refused and nothing is spent. A breed that fails gives the nomination back.
- DOC II foals are not part of the Cup, so the DOC II Lab has no nomination box.

## Seasons and boards

- A new season starts at **midnight Pacific time on the 1st of every month**. The all-time board never resets.
- In a season, one horse counts for at most **100 race points** (the all-time board has no cap).
- A season counts what cabinet saves recorded during it. A horse first saved deep into a career is measured
  from that first save, so an old unsaved career can't be dumped into one month.
- Tabs: **All**, plus each release once 5 stables score on it (Rev C, Rev D, DOC '99; DOC 2000 joins when it
  has 5). Lab foals minted as "combined" race on Rev C, so they count on the Rev C tab.
- The boards rebuild **every hour at :15**, so a new result can take up to an hour to show.
- Each stable's row shows how many horses it kept vs culled (for barns of 20+) and any former stable names.

## Fair play

- **The breeder keeps the credit forever.** The breeder of record is fixed the first time the Cup sees the
  horse race (the cabinet's seat-verified breeder tag, then the birth ledger, then the first gift's sender,
  then the holder). Gifts and trades move the horse, never the points.
- **Culled racers keep what they earned.** A horse you raced and then culled still counts. Horses the
  operator removed (forged births, quarantines) do not.
- **Clean lines only:** the same hourly check the Hall of Fame uses. A horse with an unverified horse in its
  traceable line doesn't score.
- **Imported horses (Player Card Vault arrivals) never score.** They weren't bred here.
- **Counts, never win rates.** Saving only your wins gains nothing.
- Banned stables don't appear.

## Achievements

First Winner (a horse you bred won), Stakes Breeder (won a G1), Deep Barn (10 different winners bred),
Title Factory (your horses hold 25 G1 titles between them), Bloodline (a foal of a horse you bred won),
Stud Farm (5 horses you bred won for other stables), Called It (a committed foal born with a 50 won its first
race), Season Champion (first in a monthly season), Grand Champion (first on the all-time board).

## Why it changed

The old Cup scored each horse's birth seal. The Lab lets anyone reroll until the seal they want comes up, so
the board rewarded patience at the breed button more than anything done on the track. v2 keeps seals
meaningful (a better birth makes a better racehorse, and a committed one pays a bonus), but the points come
from racing the horses you breed.
